Privacy

Updated October 7, 2026.

Your claim and the resulting report are stored to deliver the service. Please omit personal, confidential or sensitive information. Stripe handles card details; we do not store card numbers. Treat your Checkout session and report URL as private access links.

Optional usage measurement uses a random browser-tab session identifier. The database stores a keyed hash of that identifier, a broad source category, an approved campaign name, service and event timestamps. It does not store claim text, email, full referring URLs or IP addresses in the usage tables. Raw usage rows are retained for up to 30 days and expire on subsequent activity; expired rows are excluded from reporting. Infrastructure access logs are separate.

Measurement does not identify a person or prove that a visitor is an external customer. Server-confirmed payment and delivery are measured separately from browser events. You may disable session measurement using the control at the bottom of a service page. We also honor browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. A local opt-out preference persists in your browser; clearing browser storage resets it. Financial and delivery records remain necessary even when session measurement is off.

Questions or removal requests: contact@aisoup.net.
