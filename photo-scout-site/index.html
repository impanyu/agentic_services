For agents, too.

The same discovery service returns structured coordinates, visual evidence, photo tips, confidence and image provenance.

POST https://api.aisoup.net/photo-scout/v1/discover
Content-Type: application/json

{"lat":41.8827,"lon":-87.6233,"radius":1000,"limit":3}

For manual selection, first POST the same coordinates and radius to /photo-scout/v1/pois, then pass its poiCatalogToken and chosen selectedPoiIds to discover. Omit both for automatic selection.
