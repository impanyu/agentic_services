Real images. Honest limits.

Google Street View, Panoramax open street-level imagery and geolocated Wikimedia Commons photos are available. Coverage depends on the selected area. Mapillary street-level imagery can be enabled with a provider token. The model scores selected Google Street View images on the server. Google results link to the corresponding panorama and camera direction; Image providers are identified on their result cards with attribution; Google images are not cached. Map pins use independently sourced POI coordinates; provider imagery and attribution appear in the cards below.

Each result links to the original photograph, creator and license. If the images do not support a useful recommendation, we say so.

For agents, too.

The same discovery service returns structured coordinates, visual evidence, photo tips, confidence and image provenance.

POST https://api.aisoup.net/photo-scout/v1/discover
Content-Type: application/json

{"lat":41.8827,"lon":-87.6233,"radius":1000,"limit":3}

For manual selection, first POST the same coordinates and radius to /photo-scout/v1/pois, use photoStyles (e.g. ["nature"]) for a photo mood, then pass the same styles, its poiCatalogToken and chosen selectedPoiIds to discover. Omit both for automatic selection. Catalogs expire after one hour. Pay per call using the gateway's MPP payment challenge. OpenAPI ↗ · Service manifest ↗
