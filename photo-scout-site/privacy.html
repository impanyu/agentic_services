Your location choices

Updated October 8, 2026. Dream Workshop LLC operates Photo Scout.

We ask for a selected map coordinate and photo preferences. We do not request your device location. Do not include private addresses or personal information in preferences.

Coordinates are sent to configured imagery providers and OpenStreetMap to find nearby images and places. Preferences, image metadata and selected photographs are sent to OpenAI for visual analysis. Our agent traces and model response storage are disabled. Provider handling follows each provider's own policies.

Interactive OpenStreetMap tiles load directly in your browser; the tile provider receives your IP address and tile requests. Viewing original photos may also disclose your IP to image providers.

Paid requests, coordinates, preferences, report metadata and recommendations are stored in our database for report delivery. Report access expires after 30 days; expired records are removed on subsequent checkout creation. Source image bytes are used transiently for analysis and are not saved in the report database.

Your private report token is hashed on the server and kept in the original browser tab's sessionStorage. Card payment details are handled by Stripe; we retain the checkout session reference and price. Provider and payment systems may retain their own records.

For deletion or privacy questions, contact Dream Workshop and include your report ID, without sending your private report token.
