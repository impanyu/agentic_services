Your location choices

Updated October 8, 2026. Dream Workshop LLC operates Photo Scout.

We ask for a selected map coordinate and photo preferences. If you click “Use my current location”, your browser requests permission to obtain your device location. We use a single location reading to fill the map and coordinates; we do not continuously track you. Selecting your location loads map tiles for that area. Submitting a search sends the selected coordinates to our service and configured providers as described below. Do not include private addresses or personal information in preferences.

Coordinates are sent to configured imagery providers (including Wikimedia Commons, Google Street View, Panoramax and optionally Mapillary) and OpenStreetMap to find nearby images and places. Preferences, image metadata and selected photographs are sent to OpenAI for visual analysis. Our agent traces and model response storage are disabled. Provider handling follows each provider's own policies.

Interactive map tiles and styles load directly from OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap or USGS in your browser; the selected map provider receives your IP address and requests for the viewed map area. Viewing original photos may also disclose your IP to image providers.

Paid requests, coordinates, preferences, report metadata and recommendations are stored in our database for report delivery. Report access expires after 30 days; expired records are removed by the background worker or on subsequent submissions. Source image bytes are used transiently for analysis and are not saved in the report database.

Free website searches run in a server queue; delivery data expires after 24 hours. The active task token stays only in page memory. Completed searches (up to 30), their input text, selected area, POI metadata, scores and display selections are saved in browser session storage for guests, and in your account database after Google sign-in. Images and report tokens are not stored there. Switching tabs does not stop processing. Refreshing loses an unfinished task, but restores completed history and selected map layers. Guest history ends with the browser session; signed-in history follows your Google account. OAuth requests only basic identity (openid, email, profile), without Drive or other Google data access. We store an application session, name and email; Google access tokens are not retained. Daily usage counters are retained to limit requests.

Natural-language requests send the question and selected map coordinate to OpenAI to interpret search intent, with model response storage disabled. Extracted place names are sent to Photon/OpenStreetMap for geocoding. The original question is not stored as server query history; completed searches retain it in the guest browser session or signed-in account history. Payment providers may retain their own records.

Photo studio: submitting an uploaded portrait sends it and the chosen background to OpenAI for image editing. We validate and strip upload metadata. Uploads are held privately only while queued or processing and are removed on completion or failure; all preview records and generated images expire after one hour. The generated photo is an AI composite, not proof of an actual visit. Personal uploads, job access tokens and generated photos are not added to search history. Download the result before it expires. OpenAI handling follows its API data policies.

For deletion or privacy questions, contact Dream Workshop and include your report ID, without sending your private report token.
