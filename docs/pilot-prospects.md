# Public pilot prospects — review before outreach

Researched October 7, 2026. These are possible discovery/interview targets,
**not customers, qualified leads, endorsements or confirmed buyers**. Their public
work supports the role description; fit for Web Evidence is our hypothesis.
Established publications may be difficult to reach and may already have better
verification workflows. Prefer warm introductions and smaller independent writers
when available. No contact list has been purchased or private address collected.

## Prepared invitation

Use the short invitation in [the pilot plan](first-paid-customer-pilot.md), adjusted
to the relevant angle above. Do not claim familiarity with someone's work without
reviewing an actual recent article. Do not send a bulk campaign, use a privacy-only
contact channel for marketing, subscribe the recipient to a mailing list, or buy
sponsorship. Public contact availability is not agreement to try or buy.

Outreach remains unsent pending explicit authorization naming recipients/channel.
After a reply, record consent and actual task fit before calling anyone qualified.
The acceptance target remains one independently confirmed external live payment and
a useful delivered report, not merely an answer to an invitation.
