Privacy Policy

Updated October 6, 2026.

We collect permitted signals from configured external sources to identify market needs. Editors review candidates before publication. Evaluations may show reviewed source links, observation dates and aggregate measures; raw candidate excerpts remain private.

We retain collected evidence according to each source's access terms and retention requirements. To request removal, email contact@aisoup.net with the source URL or evaluation ID so we can locate related records.

On-demand research stores your search criteria, progress and results for up to 30 days. A hashed subscriber identifier controls access and daily quotas. Queries are research objectives, not submitted demand evidence. Please do not include personal or confidential information. Our research agent uses OpenAI and approved source tools to analyze objectives and evidence. Unpublished results are available only through your authenticated research request; independently supported market assessments may later enter the public catalog.

Stripe processes subscription payments. We store Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the checkout email address, subscription status, and a hash of the access token to deliver and control access. We do not store card numbers. Subscribers can manage their subscription through the Stripe customer portal.

Editors choose and review configured public sources. Source platforms may change or remove content. We will review related records when we receive a reasonable removal request.
